2. How we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the Service you asked for: run your requests through the models, store your history and files, enforce limits. (Where data protection law asks for a legal basis: performance of our agreement with you.)
  • To keep the Service secure and working: logs, backups, investigating incidents and misuse. (Legitimate interests in operating a secure service.)
  • To meet legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests or reporting illegal content. (Legal obligation.)

We do not use your content to train or fine-tune models, do not sell or rent personal data, do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who can access your data

  • The operator. The web app is configured so administrators cannot open or export members' chats from within it. The operator still has technical access to the server and database. We access stored content only when you ask us to (for example for support), to investigate a security incident or a serious breach of the Acceptable Use Policy, or when required by law.
  • Service providers that help run the Service, under their own terms:
    • Tailscale Inc. provides the private network members connect through. It sees connection metadata (devices, network addresses, when they connect), not the content of your traffic, which is encrypted end to end between your device and our server.
    • Cloudflare, Inc., once public access moves to it, will route web traffic through its network. Cloudflare terminates the encrypted connection at its edge, so it can technically see traffic passing through it.
    • Amazon Web Services stores our backups, encrypted with a key we control.
  • Authorities, when we are legally required to disclose data, or where necessary to prevent serious harm.

The models and the software run on our own server. Model developers (for example Qwen or OpenAI) receive no data from Aaru.

4. Where data is stored

On our server and, for encrypted backups, with Amazon Web Services in the United States. If you are outside the country where our server is located, your data is transferred there to provide the Service.

5. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, encrypted off-site backups, separate database accounts for each component, regular security audits and software updates. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and any authority as the law requires.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: you can export your chats in the web app (Settings → Data Controls). Ask us for a copy of other data we hold about you.
  • Correction: update your profile in the web app, or ask us.
  • Deletion: delete individual chats and files yourself, or ask us to delete your account. Deleted data leaves backups as they expire (at most 12 months); if a backup is ever restored, we re-apply deletions.
  • Objection and restriction, and other rights that the law where you live gives you, including rights under the GDPR and US state privacy laws where they apply.

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will answer within 30 days and may need to confirm it is you. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

7. Children

The Service is for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect data from children; if we learn we have, we will delete it.
