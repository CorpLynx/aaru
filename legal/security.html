Reporting a vulnerability

Email {{SECURITY_EMAIL}} with what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it affects. Please do not include other members' data in the report. We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days and to keep you updated until the issue is resolved.

Good-faith research

If you act in good faith under this policy, we will not pursue or support legal action against you for your research, and we consider it authorised under the Acceptable Use Policy. In return:

  • Test only against your own account and data. Do not access, change or delete other members' data; if you reach any by accident, stop, and tell us.
  • Do not degrade the Service: no denial-of-service, load or volume testing, and no social engineering or physical attacks.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly. We will agree a date with you.

This is a private, non-commercial service; there is no bug bounty.
